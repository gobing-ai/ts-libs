# O3: Gemini Decision 1 and GPT-6 Luna DecisionMaker drivers

## Goal

Enable Google's Gemini Decision 1 (`gemini-decision-1`) and OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna Decisions (`gpt-6-luna`) as provider-neutral `DecisionDriver` implementations so callers can target both hosted decision endpoints through `createDecisionMaker({ driver })` and `DecisionMakerRegistry`.

## Scope

- In: `packages/decision-gemini` (`@gobing-ai/ts-decision-gemini`, `gemini-decision-1`, `POST /v1beta/decisions`, `x-goog-api-key`) and `packages/decision-openai` (`@gobing-ai/ts-decision-openai`, `gpt-6-luna`, `POST /v1/decisions`, `Authorization: Bearer`) over `@gobing-ai/ts-infra` `APIClient` and `@gobing-ai/ts-ai-runner` validators/errors.
- In: `choice`, `score`, and `noul` question/answer primitives, explicit credential/transport options (including `fetch` injection for tests), `DecisionMakerRegistry` custom registration, decision-boundary rules, and ADR-042 / package documentation.
- Out: Adding external vendor SDKs (`@google/genai` or `openai`) when `APIClient` REST suffices, automatic environment discovery, or changing `DecisionMakerRegistry` built-ins.
- Out: Changing `@gobing-ai/ts-ai-runner`'s public `DecisionMaker` / `Question` / `Answer` contract for non-JSON multimodal attachments (images/audio/video/PDFs), or publishing packages to npm.
